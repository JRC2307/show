guide

Start in one minute
  1. Open this page. It already moves by itself, slowly, and never repeats.
  2. Pick a scene with keys 1 to 9 and 0, and a color with the arrow keys.
  3. Press open output (under setup), drag the new window to the projector and press f in it.
  4. To make it follow the music, press sound off and allow the microphone. Otherwise it breathes by itself.
  5. Leave it. Every scene is made to be watched for hours without touching anything.
Calm scenes
  • 1 fluid: slow ink folding over itself.
  • 2 roots: thousands of fine tips climb and join into thick roots that keep moving.
  • 3 mycelium: a dense web, like fungus under the soil.
  • 4 veins: an open network of paths looking for each other.
  • 5 coral: thick branches that grow and pull back.
  • 6 contours: the height lines of a landscape that keeps changing.
  • 7 wood: the rings of a trunk that is still growing.
  • 8 mud: cracked earth with light in the cracks.
  • 9 sand: dunes with the ripples the wind leaves.
  • 0 lichen: colonies that spread and shrink on stone.
  • q forest: an endless walk past a misty wood, simple shapes and soft light. It is the brightest scene, so it suits a wooden wall.
  • v video: your own video as the background, tinted with the chosen color, with the outlines of its light drawn on top. Press load video and pick the file; it loops without sound. shape makes it ripple.
  • Between two scenes the picture fades to black and back. The four living scenes (2 to 5) turn into each other without a cut.
Wild scenes
  • Hold shift and press a number: tunnel, cavern, kaleido, acid, eyes, spiral, moiré, julia, lava and truchet.
  • They follow the music more closely and move fast, but they never flash: fast flashing can trigger seizures in people with epilepsy.
  • A click, the space bar and auto-change stay inside the group you are in: calm stays calm, wild stays wild.
  • The last three colors suit them best: neon, ultra and acid.
Color and controls
  • Six earth colors: earth, moss, clay, river, bone and ember, and three strong ones: neon, ultra and acid.
  • wall boost: lightens the whole picture, mostly the middle tones. Turn it up when the wall soaks up the light (wood, brick, a dark wall); turn it down on a screen or a white wall.
  • response: how strongly the picture follows the music. At zero it does not listen.
  • speed: how fast everything moves.
  • shape: changes the drawing. Each scene uses it in its own way (more lines, smaller cracks, wider roots).
  • dimmer: overall brightness (- and =). It starts at full, so it only goes down.
Sound and auto-change
  • sound off: the picture breathes by itself. Press it and it says listening: it follows the audio input you choose in the list. Bass moves the picture and volume adds light. Nothing flashes or cuts.
  • A cable from the DJ mixer into an audio interface works best. The laptop microphone also works.
  • hold scene (a): the scene stays until you change it. Press it to change scene automatically every 5, 15 or 30 minutes.
  • blackout (b): fades the picture to black. Press it again to bring it back.
Text
  • Type a word or a name (enter jumps to the box) and press show text.
  • In the living scenes (2 to 5) the word grows: it takes a few seconds to form. In the other scenes it appears on top.
A second computer, or a window with no panel
  • On the other computer open this same page with ?out=1 at the end, or serve the file there the same way and press open output.
  • One click on the picture goes to the next scene.
  • Move the mouse and a bar appears with the scenes, the colors, load video, sound, auto-change, blackout, fullscreen and map. It hides by itself.
  • Each computer remembers its own scene, color and map.
  • If a panel is open on the same computer, the output window follows it: scene, color, controls and the loaded video.
Mapping: fit the picture to walls and objects
  • Press open output, move that window to the projector and make it fullscreen (f). Then press map in the panel, or p in the projector window.
  • A surface appears with a grid and four corners. With the mouse over the projection, drag each corner to the real corner of the wall, the screen or the box.
  • Fine tuning: click a corner and move it with the arrow keys (one step at a time; ten with shift). tab goes to the next corner.
  • Drag from inside to move the whole surface. + surface (n) adds another, up to eight. remove deletes the selected one.
  • fit puts the whole picture inside that surface. window (v) uses it as a cut-out: you see the part of the picture behind it, so several surfaces share one large picture.
  • grid (g) switches between the alignment grid and the real picture. Everything outside the surfaces is black.
  • done (esc) finishes. The map is saved on this computer and comes back when you open the output.
  • save file downloads the map; load file brings it back on another computer or after the browser data is cleared. Save it once the setup is finished.
  • If the projector moves, the corners need adjusting again. clear all (press twice) goes back to the picture with no map.
  • With a single screen: map in the panel lets you adjust right there, and the map applies when you hide the panel (h).
Setting up at the venue
  • No network. The page is a single file. Download it to the laptop, run python3 -m http.server in that folder and open localhost:8000 in Chrome. Do not rely on the venue's wifi.
  • Two windows. The panel stays on the laptop screen. The output window goes to the projector over HDMI, fullscreen.
  • One screen. Use this same window: h hides the panel and f makes it fullscreen.
  • The output window must stay visible. If it is minimized or covered, it stops drawing.
  • Any computer. The page measures how long each frame takes and lowers the detail by itself until it runs smoothly. It remembers that for next time.
MIDI controller
  • Plug in the controller and press midi (under setup). Allow access.
  • To assign a control: press midi learn, then the button or the control name here, then move or press the control on the device.
  • By default notes 36 to 57 pick the scene (calm, forest, video, wild) and controls 21 to 25 are the five sliders.

If something goes wrong
  • The sound bars do not move: the chosen input is not the one carrying the music. Pick another from the list, or check the microphone permission behind the padlock in the address bar.
  • The output does not follow the panel: both windows must be in the same Chrome and at the same address. Close the output and open it again with the button.
  • The output shows the video scene without the video: wait two seconds, it asks the panel for the file by itself. If it still does not appear, press load video again.
  • The output froze: it is covered or minimized. Bring it to the front.
  • It runs slowly: the page lowers the detail by itself within a few seconds. Closing other tabs and plugging in the laptop helps.
  • Everything is stuck: reload the page. The scene, the color and the controls are kept.
